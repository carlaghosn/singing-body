TA muscle
CT muscle

TA (thyroarytenoid) is the muscle inside the folds. It shortens and thickens them: chest voice, lower notes. CT (cricothyroid) sits on the front of the voice box. It tilts the cartilage to stretch the folds longer and thinner: higher notes, head voice. Mix = both working together.
